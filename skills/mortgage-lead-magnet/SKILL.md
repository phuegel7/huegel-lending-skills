# Mortgage Lead Magnet Builder (Huegel Lending)

Use this when Pete asks for a lead magnet, freebie, opt-in offer, landing page for a download, or a quiz or calculator that captures leads. The goal is a free resource a borrower or real estate agent wants enough to share contact details, that leads naturally to a conversation with Pete.

## Who this is for
- Pete Huegel, loan originator, NMLS #242395, Huegel Lending powered by Barrett Financial Group. Licensed in Arizona, California and Texas. Lends across all of Arizona (do not frame as Phoenix-only or Chandler-based).
- Primary audiences: first-time and move-up buyers, self-employed and complex-scenario borrowers, people considering a HELOC, relocation buyers moving to Arizona, and real estate agents (referral partners).
- Loan types Pete can speak to: conventional, FHA, VA, jumbo, Non-QM, and down payment assistance (DPA). Never promise a borrower will qualify for DPA or any program.

## Voice
- Warm, conversational, confident, locally grounded, relationship-first, never pushy. Lead with education, not sales.
- Plain language: contractions, no unexplained acronyms (LTV, DTI), rounded dollar figures, numbered steps.
- Soft, empowering call to action on educational pieces only.
- Never invent client stories, testimonials, results, or statistics. If a number is needed, mark it [VERIFY] and tell Pete.

## Step 1: Pick one problem and one audience
Ask Pete (or choose and state the assumption) for: audience, single problem solved, and the next step it leads to (call, pre-qualification, agent partnership). One magnet = one problem.

## Step 2: Choose the format
| Format | Best for | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| Checklist | Quick win, beginners (e.g. documents to gather before applying) | Low |
| Short guide | Education and trust (e.g. how self-employed income is evaluated) | Medium |
| Quiz | Sorting leads by readiness or loan type | Medium |
| Calculator | Affordability or payment estimates | Medium |
| Agent co-branded one-pager | Referral partner growth | Low |

Sample concepts: Arizona First-Time Buyer Document Checklist; Pre-Approval Readiness Quiz; Self-Employed Borrower Income Guide; How Much Home Can I Afford (payment estimator); Moving to Arizona Buyer Roadmap; Down Payment Help: What Programs Exist (educational, no eligibility promises); Agent Cheat Sheet: What I Need From You to Get Your Buyer Approved.

Quoted conversion rates from generic marketing playbooks are unverified. Do not promise or cite expected conversion numbers.

## Step 3: Produce the package
Output these sections:
1. Overview: title, format, audience, problem solved, time to consume.
2. The lead magnet content itself, complete and usable.
3. Landing page copy: headline options (benefit-led), 3 outcome bullets, short preview of what is inside, 2 to 3 FAQ items (privacy, time, cost), form and button text.
4. Delivery email: subject, short body, one soft next step.
5. Follow-up: 3 short nurture email ideas that continue the education.
6. Disclosures block (see below) and a [VERIFY] list of anything needing Pete's confirmation.

## Form rules
- Ask for only what will be used. Email only is the default; add first name if the follow-up uses it.
- Add phone only if Pete wants calls or texts, and include clear consent language near the field. Flag to Pete that phone and text consent rules should be checked with his compliance contact at Barrett.
- Do not collect SSN, income, or other sensitive data in a lead magnet form.

## Compliance guardrails (flag, do not guess)
- No specific rates, APRs, payments, or payment terms in ads or landing copy without Pete confirming the required disclosures. When unsure, leave the figure out and mark [VERIFY].
- No guarantees of approval, savings, or program eligibility. Use "may", "could", and "depends on".
- Calculators and estimators must say results are estimates, not a loan offer or commitment.
- Fair housing: no language that targets or excludes people by protected characteristics or neighborhoods.
- Final copy should be reviewed by Pete (and compliance if required by Barrett) before publishing.

## Disclosures
Every published page includes Pete's standing footer: licensing and Equal Housing block (NMLS #242395, Barrett Financial Group, L.L.C. NMLS #181106, state license details, Equal Housing Lender) plus this disclaimer: "This is not a commitment to lend. All loans are subject to credit approval, underwriting, and property appraisal. Rates and terms are subject to change without notice. Licensing information is updated periodically and may not reflect the most recent changes." Use the exact footer text Pete has saved for Huegel Lending websites; if it is not available, insert a [PASTE FOOTER] placeholder rather than rewriting it.

## Delivery and tracking
- Deliver by email or a hosted page; if the lead goes into Viato, suggest a tag per magnet so follow-up can be segmented by topic.
- Test the headline and button text first. Judge results from real numbers in Viato, not from generic benchmarks.
